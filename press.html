What is this?

Not a rushed game collection

99 AI Games is a long-term public archive for observing AI coding-agent progress through playable browser-game experiments. The current archive is early by design: it starts with real observation samples and preserves how the work changes over time.

Why games?

Games expose more than static demos

Games reveal design judgment, rules, feedback, input feel, debugging, polish, accessibility, and creative limitations. Playability makes the AI's work inspectable: visitors can open a sample, feel the controls, inspect the source, and read the provenance.

Provenance rules

Truth over polish

  • No fake screenshots.
  • No fake popularity metrics.
  • No fake provenance.
  • Playable builds only count as playable samples.
  • Run records describe what happened, including pending checks.
  • Model and tool labels are recorded directly in metadata.
  • The human code edit policy is explicit.

Roadmap

Careful continuation

Future work should continue adding and remaking observation samples carefully, preserving old variants instead of pretending they never existed. The roadmap is not a claim that 99 games are complete.
