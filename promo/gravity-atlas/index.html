Gravity Atlas

A PC-first deterministic physics puzzle. Drag from the launch pad to aim and fire a probe across six gravity plates, curving its path through masses, repulsors, and void zones into the target ring within each plate's shot budget. The physics engine is a shared pure module, and a committed Node verifier replays embedded reference launch vectors to prove every plate is completable.
