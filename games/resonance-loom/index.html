HOW TO PLAY

Controls

DFJK  hit the 4 lanes (or tap the lane on touch screens)
Space  pause / resume
Esc  quit to menu

Goal

Notes fall down four lanes. Press the matching key the instant a note crosses the glowing line near the bottom. The closer to the line, the better your judgement.

Scoring

Perfect · 300 pts   Great · 200 pts   Good · 100 pts   Miss · 0, breaks combo
Build combo for a score multiplier. Clear the song for a grade from D to S.

Audio

All music is synthesised live in your browser by the Web Audio API — original melodies, no copyrighted audio. Hit notes accurately to add their tones to the weave.
