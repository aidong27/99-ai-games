# Observation 010 / Game 010: Resonance Loom

## Hall

Rhythm Audio Hall

## Observation focus

Resonance Loom tests whether an AI coding agent can coordinate timing windows,
four-lane input, live synthesized music, scoring feedback, and responsive touch
controls in a dependency-free browser game.

## Play loop

1. Choose one of three original songs.
2. Hit falling notes with D, F, J, and K or tap the lanes.
3. Build combo through Perfect, Great, and Good timing judgements.
4. Finish the chart to receive an S through D grade.
5. Replay to improve the locally stored high score.
