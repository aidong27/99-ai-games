Observation 002 / Game 002

Light the lattice

Press any node to fire light down its row and column. Light every node to win.

Rules

  • Every node starts lit or dark.
  • Pressing a node flips its full row and column.
  • The pressed node flips once, with the cross.
  • Light every node to synchronize the lattice.

Controls

  • Press a node: click / tap
  • Move cursor: WASD / Arrow keys
  • Fire node: Space or Enter
  • Hint: H · Undo: U · Reset: R
