# Game 002 Brief: Lumen Lattice — Prism Archive

## Concept

A PC-first constraint logic puzzle for the Puzzle Logic Hall. The board is a square
lattice of **prisms** that are either aligned (lit) or dark. Pressing a prism sends
light through a **fixed pattern** decided by the level's active rule, flipping a set of
prisms. The goal is to align every prism and synchronize the lattice.

This is the **Prism Archive remake** of Game 002, recorded as a new canonical variant
(`claude-lumen-lattice-prism-archive-2026-06-03`) under the same game slot. The original
variant (`claude-opus-4-8-2026-05-31`) and its run records are preserved.

## Rules

Each press applies a deterministic transformation (a linear toggle over GF(2)):

- **Pulse** — flips the pressed prism and its four orthogonal neighbours.
- **Beam** — flips every prism in the pressed prism's row and column.
- **Split** — flips the pressed prism and its four diagonal neighbours.

Rules are introduced one at a time: lattice 1 teaches Pulse, and each new rule gets a
short one-line note the first time it appears.

## Why this is a Puzzle Logic Hall observation sample

The hall observes AI ability in rules, constraints, state logic, solvability, and puzzle
communication. Prism Archive makes those qualities inspectable:

- **Solvability is guaranteed, not hoped for.** Every lattice is generated by
  reverse-scrambling a fully aligned board with random presses. Because each press is its
  own inverse and presses commute, re-applying the tracked press set always restores the
  solved board. An unsolvable lattice cannot be generated.
- **The solver is part of the design.** The generator tracks the parity of the scramble
  presses, updated in place on every player move, so the hint always points at a prism in
  a valid remaining solution, and `window.__lumenLatticeQA.autoSolve()` finishes any board.
- **Win detection is independent of the tracked solution** (won = every prism aligned), so
  alternative solutions still count while the hint path stays valid.
- **Readable feedback** shows which prisms a press flipped (a brief ring), the beam sweep
  for the Beam rule, the current rule, the directive, and live aligned-count progress.

## Scope

- 6 lattices (4x4 to 6x6) across three rules, with growing scramble depth and per-lattice par.
- PC-first: centered board tuned for 1440x900 and 1920x1080, full keyboard + mouse control.
- Mouse/tap to press, keyboard cursor (WASD/Arrows) with Space/Enter to activate, H hint,
  U undo, R reset, P pause/menu.
- No external assets, no build step, no network calls.
