# Observation 003 Brief: Neon Pulse Courier

## Purpose

Create a PC-first arcade reaction benchmark for the Arcade Reaction Hall. The observation focuses on feedback, timing, input feel, difficulty curve, short-loop clarity, combo pressure, and readable danger states.

## Core Loop

1. Start a 75-second courier run.
2. Move through a rhythmic pulse arena.
3. Collect data parcels for score.
4. Maintain combo while avoiding pulse barriers.
5. Use dash to cross tight timing windows.
6. Survive until extraction or fail when integrity reaches zero.

## Design Constraints

- Keep the experience short, readable, and replayable.
- Use a PC-first 16:9 playfield.
- Prefer clear visual warning states over surprise damage.
- Keep mobile honest as a baseline viewport check, not the intended high-skill input mode.
- Do not claim fake screenshots or physical handset QA.

## Archive Role

This is Observation 003 / Game 003 and the first Arcade Reaction Hall benchmark sample. It should not be counted as a new variant of another game or as a placeholder slot.
