# Game Halls

The 99 AI Games archive is organized into 9 halls. Each hall is an AI capability observation category, not just a genre bucket. Each hall can eventually contain 11 official observation samples, for a total of 99 game slots.

Game numbers are global observation samples. Hall order does not force numeric ranges. For example, Observation 001 / Game 001 belongs to the Survival Strategy Hall because that is the best fit for Signal Cartographer.

## Observation Focus

- Arcade Reaction Hall observes AI ability in feedback, timing, input feel, difficulty curves, and short-loop clarity.
- Puzzle Logic Hall observes AI ability in rules, constraints, state logic, solvability, and puzzle communication.
- Survival Strategy Hall observes AI ability in resource pressure, route planning, risk feedback, and tactical systems.
- Card Strategy Hall observes AI ability in probabilistic decisions, drafting, turn structure, and readable strategy.
- Text Adventure Hall observes AI ability in branching narrative, state memory, tone, and meaningful choices.
- Clicker Management Hall observes AI ability in economies, automation loops, scaling, and player motivation.
- Physics Experiment Hall observes AI ability in simulation feel, spatial reasoning, cause/effect, and sandbox tuning.
- Rhythm Audio Hall observes AI ability in timing windows, pattern memory, audio feedback, and input precision.
- AI Meme Hall observes AI ability in self-reference, humor, weird failure modes, and playful systems.

## Slot Mix

Each hall should eventually include:

- 3 benchmark observation samples
- 6 normal observation samples
- 1 failed/weird experiment observation sample
- 1 future remake observation sample

Across 9 halls, that creates:

- 27 benchmark observation samples
- 54 normal observation samples
- 9 failed/weird observation samples
- 9 future remake observation samples
- 99 total observation samples
