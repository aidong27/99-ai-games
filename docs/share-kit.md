# 99 AI Games Share Kit

## One-Line Pitch

99 AI Games gives different AI coding systems the same locked browser-game
challenge, then publishes real playable results and verification evidence.

## Short English Intro

99 AI Games is a public playable benchmark: same brief, same rules, different
AI. Every formal Entry builds Protocol 99, runs in real Chromium, and keeps its
prompt, source, browser evidence, and provenance inspectable.

## 中文简介

99 AI Games 是一个公开可玩的 AI 编程系统观察项目：同一份提示词、同一套
规则，不同 AI 完成同一个 Protocol 99 浏览器游戏挑战。每个正式作品保留
提示词哈希、源码、真实浏览器证据和来源记录。

## Posting Rules

- Say “AI coding system” when referring to model + Agent + tool environment.
- Do not call Automated Compliance an intelligence or scientific score.
- Do not claim 99 Entries are complete.
- Do not invent users, downloads, votes, ratings, or popularity.
- Do not replace pending screenshots with generated gameplay.
- Keep Raw and Repair results labeled separately.
- Keep Legacy and Protocol 99 counts separate.
